V.03 / IABuyer architecture

Object · house · trial · guided discovery

An information architecture with more than one way into scent.

Violet does not assume fragrance vocabulary. Buyers can enter through objects, maisons, trial ritual or a guided Scent Portrait, while utility commerce stays conventional and easy to recover.

01 / Global navigation

Four discovery models, one quiet utility layer.

Search remains utility rather than a primary category. Bag is always reachable, while Fragrances, Houses, Discovery and Scent Portrait represent distinct user mental models.

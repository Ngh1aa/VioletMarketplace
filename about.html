V.40 / ABOUT VIOLET

We built a fragrance space around the pause before choosing.

01 / WHY VIOLET

Discovery should feel like entering a room.

Most product catalogues expose everything at once. Violet does the opposite: image before metadata, feeling before filtering, trial before commitment. The project is fictional, but the interaction model tests a real design question — how can fragrance commerce leave room for uncertainty?

A scent can be technically correct and still not belong to you. Time is part of the interface.
02 / THE EDIT

Fewer objects. More context.

The fictional maisons are not there to imitate real brands. They let Violet prototype house-led discovery: each one carries a material vocabulary, a pace and a territory. The catalogue stays intentionally small so the experience can behave more like a curated room than an infinite shelf.

03 / THE RITUAL

Wear. Wait. Return.

The interaction system repeats one idea across the site: do not force certainty too early. Save three directions, let the drydown change your mind, then come back with better context. In this prototype the state lives only in the browser; no real samples, payments or fulfilment are processed.

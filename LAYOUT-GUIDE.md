# Violet — Exploration-first Layout Contract

## Visual foundation
- Canvas: `#FFFFFF` is the dominant page background.
- Primary focal colour: pastel violet `#E8DCF2` with soft field `#F6F1F9` and stronger pastel `#D8C3E8`.
- Aubergine `#382C42` is supporting depth, not the dominant surface.
- Buttons default to pastel violet with dark aubergine text; strong aubergine is reserved for selected high-contrast moments.

## Three global rules
1. Prefer asymmetry to repeated equal grids.
2. Give images more area than copy; preserve generous negative space.
3. Reveal progressively rather than exposing every commercial detail immediately.

## Image : copy targets
- Home hero: 85:15.
- Editorial story: 55:45.
- Product cards: 75:25 or more image.
- PDP opening: 60:40.
- Maison: 70:30.
- Texture / visual pause: 95:5 or 100:0.
- Closing CTA: 40:60 on a solid colour field.
- Desktop text line length: never intentionally exceed 75 characters.

## Page contracts
### Home
Image-led asymmetric hero → large philosophy statement → staggered product edit → full-bleed visual pause → exploratory routes → houses → pastel-violet CTA.

### Library
No permanent Amazon-style left rail. Refinements are hidden by default and opened only when requested. Product prices are secondary and revealed on hover/focus. Product layout uses uneven spans and editorial interruptions.

### PDP
Bounded 60:40 opening composition. Media remains sticky only inside the opening product composition. Story and related content begin after that boundary. Supporting imagery is mood/material, not fake extra packshots.

### Houses
House index is horizontal scroll with snap points to feel like moving through rooms. House detail opens with a near-full-screen visual field; context is revealed after the image establishes place.

### Discovery / Scent Portrait
Sections use near-full viewport height. Reflection and choice are primary. Progress is minimal, and pastel-violet fields respond as journey markers rather than generic form chrome.

### About
Editorial magazine rhythm: large image fields alternate with offset text compositions and a large breathing statement.

## Interaction principles
- Intentional cropping is preferred to always showing a complete product immediately.
- Hover may reveal alternate material/atmosphere imagery and delayed price information.
- Headlines should invite continuation rather than announce inventory.
- Secondary CTAs should sound exploratory.
- Long detail is progressively revealed when possible.
- Insert visual pauses at least every 3–4 content sections.

## Prototype boundary
Violet remains a fictional portfolio prototype. No UI should imply real stock, shipping, payments, partner relationships, provenance systems or AI personality prediction.

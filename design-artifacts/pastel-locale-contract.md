# Violet Pastel + Locale Contract

## Language and currency
- Primary interface language: English
- Prototype catalogue currency: EUR
- Display format: `160 €`
- Footer market label: `International edit · EUR · Curated prototype`

## Brand colour roles
- Pastel violet is a visible system colour, not an incidental image colour.
- Pastel violet appears in section fields, selected states, filter chips, PDP buying surfaces, Discovery/Finder panels and utility summaries.
- Dark aubergine remains the primary high-contrast luxury accent for top chrome, strong CTAs and editorial interruption fields.
- Ivory remains a supporting neutral rather than the dominant identity by itself.

## Core palette
- Lavender field: `#F3EEF7`
- Soft lilac: `#F1EAF5`
- Pastel violet: `#E7DDEF`
- Strong lilac: `#D4C0E0`
- Violet accent: `#775F88`
- Deep aubergine: `#3E3048`
- Ink: `#18141B`
- Paper: `#FFFDFB`

## Guardrail
Do not treat pink, beige or purple hues contained inside stock/editorial photography as evidence of the Violet brand palette. The UI itself must carry the palette.

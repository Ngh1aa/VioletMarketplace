V.04 / FLOWSDecision journeys

Browse · try · understand · commit · recover

Four ways to move from curiosity to confidence.

The flows are designed around uncertainty, not just conversion. Violet supports confident buyers, house-led explorers, people who want to try first, and users who need help translating feelings into scent language.

01 / Hero commerce flow

Browse with scent language, then choose the right level of commitment.

The PDP splits commitment into “Try first” and “Add full bottle” rather than forcing every visitor toward the same purchase decision.

04 / Discovery trio

Trial becomes a deliberate workspace, not a promo add-on.

The three-slot capacity is visible at every step. Users can remove or replace a choice before moving toward the simulated bag.

Dead ends always expose a next useful action.

Recovery is designed into filters, capacity, form validation and deep-linked routes rather than treated as an afterthought.

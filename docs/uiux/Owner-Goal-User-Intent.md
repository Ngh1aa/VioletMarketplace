# Owner Goal ↔ User Intent — Violet V4

| Owner goal | User intent | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Make Violet credible as a curated multi-house retailer | Know why this marketplace deserves trust | Show houses, provenance, authenticity/service policy and curation through structure, not repeated claims |
| Increase confident fragrance purchase | Reduce risk before committing | Make discovery/sample a first-class format and reconnect it to full bottle |
| Differentiate from generic ecommerce | Browse in a way that feels specific to perfume | Use family, mood, presence, house and sampling as navigation/merchandising primitives |
| Build memorable Violet identity | Recognize the site without relying only on purple/logo | Establish controlled media direction, composition signatures, annotation grammar and house/editorial rhythm |
| Keep shopping understandable | See price/format/action quickly | Commerce action must remain familiar and earlier than long storytelling |
| Support assisted discovery | Express preferences without perfumery expertise | Keep Scent Portrait short; explain recommendations; continue into sample/evaluation |
| Preserve prototype truth | Understand what really works | Label deterministic/local/simulated behavior; no AI/payment/auth claims |

## Priority decision sequence
1. Orientation: Fragrances / Houses / Discovery / Scent Finder.
2. Narrowing: family, mood, house, concentration or guided portrait.
3. Evaluation: scent character + notes + house + format + sample/full bottle.
4. Trial: representative sample/discovery path.
5. Reflection: simple local evaluation when V4 implements it.
6. Commitment: full bottle → bag → simulated checkout.

## Non-goals for V4 desktop prototype
- No social/community layer.
- No real accounts, loyalty, payment or inventory service.
- No pseudo-personality AI language.
- No mobile/tablet completion claim.

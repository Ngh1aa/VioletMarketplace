# Audience and Top Tasks — Violet V4

No first-party user research is available; these are project-grounded audience hypotheses to guide prototype design, not validated personas.

## A1 — Fragrance-curious shopper
Entry context: knows a feeling, occasion or broad family but not niche-perfumery vocabulary.
Top tasks:
1. understand what Violet sells and why it is curated;
2. narrow from mood/family without reading dozens of product descriptions;
3. sample before committing;
4. understand why a recommendation fits.
Primary friction in V3: sampling is separated from Scent Portrait and full-bottle decision.

## A2 — Niche-fragrance enthusiast
Entry context: may enter on PLP/PDP from a house/product query.
Top tasks:
1. browse by house, family, concentration and notes;
2. inspect provenance, format and scent profile quickly;
3. compare related directions;
4. find sample/discovery availability.
Primary friction in V3: houses are not first-class destinations and card metadata is not optimized for expert scanning.

## A3 — Gift buyer
Entry context: wants something premium but may not know the recipient’s exact scent taste.
Top tasks:
1. choose a low-risk gift format;
2. understand packaging/gift options truthfully;
3. use mood/discovery guidance;
4. complete checkout calmly.
Primary friction in V3: Gift Edit exists as taxonomy but not a guided decision flow.

## A4 — Returning sampler
Entry context: has tried one or more samples and wants to decide what to buy next.
Top tasks:
1. remember what was recommended/tried;
2. record simple like/neutral/dislike reaction;
3. refine shortlist;
4. convert to full bottle.
Current reality: this state does not exist yet. V4 may simulate it locally only if clearly labeled prototype behavior.

## Cross-audience top questions
- What kind of scent is this?
- What will it feel like on skin / in space?
- Can I try it before buying a bottle?
- Why is Violet recommending or carrying it?
- Which house made it?
- What happens after I sample it?
- What is real versus prototype behavior?

## Priority
P0 journey: discover → narrow → sample/try → evaluate → full-bottle decision.
P1 journey: browse house → fragrance → sample/full bottle.
P1 journey: gift → discovery/gift format → checkout.

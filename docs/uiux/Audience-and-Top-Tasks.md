# Audience and Top Tasks — Violet V4

No first-party analytics/interviews are available. These are explicit professional hypotheses to guide prototype design, not research claims.

## Audience A — fragrance curious

Context: knows desired feeling or broad family but not perfumery vocabulary.
Top tasks:
1. orient quickly without learning jargon;
2. narrow to a manageable shortlist;
3. understand what a scent may feel like on skin;
4. try with low commitment;
5. return to a full bottle when confidence is earned.

Primary routes: Home → Scent Portrait / Discovery → PDP.

## Audience B — niche enthusiast

Context: already understands notes/houses and wants new discoveries.
Top tasks:
1. browse maisons and new releases;
2. filter by family/mood/concentration;
3. inspect house provenance and scent structure;
4. compare nearby directions;
5. sample before buying.

Primary routes: Houses / PLP → PDP → Discovery/sample.

## Audience C — gift buyer

Context: wants a refined gift without knowing recipient’s exact signature scent.
Top tasks:
1. reduce risk;
2. understand mood/format quickly;
3. choose discovery/travel/gift format;
4. see gift-worthiness and price clearly;
5. checkout with minimal friction.

Primary routes: Home / Discovery → set/PDP → Bag → Checkout.

## Audience D — returning evaluator

Context: has already tried or shortlisted scents.
Top tasks:
1. recover previous portrait/selection where prototype state supports it;
2. evaluate sample candidates;
3. promote favorites to full-bottle consideration;
4. compare price/size/availability.

Primary routes: Scent Portrait results / Discovery → PDP → Bag.

## Top-task priority

1. Find a scent direction.
2. Understand a specific fragrance enough to decide next step.
3. Try/sample with low commitment.
4. Browse a trusted house/curated edit.
5. Purchase full bottle or discovery format.

## Design implication

The site should spend less first-screen space explaining Violet and more space exposing decision objects: scent, house, mood, sample route, price and evidence.

# Violet V4 — Visual Signature

## Existing cues to preserve

- warm ivory + aubergine + lilac family;
- editorial serif product language;
- scent-family/mood vocabulary;
- fine rules and restrained density;
- Scent Portrait as a signature interaction.

## Digital signature cues

### 1. Violet Index
Small archive labels (`V.01`, `HOUSE 02`, `OBJECT 06`, `DISCOVERY / 03`) orient rather than decorate.

### 2. Object Study Frame
Product media has a precise thin-rule frame/classification footer, not a generic card surface.

### 3. Aubergine Ink Rule
One strong line or side rule identifies active/editorial zones across PLP, PDP, Houses and Discovery.

### 4. Warm Paper / Violet Ink behavior
Warm paper is dominant; violet is concentrated in action, navigation datum, annotations and selected states.

### 5. Sensory serif sentence + factual sans metadata
A short sensory line can be expressive; specs, price, state and actions remain clear sans/UI language.

### 6. Page-role asymmetry
Home, Catalogue, PDP, House, Discovery and Finder have different silhouettes, but use the same grid/rule/type behaviors.

## Generic patterns to avoid

- violet gradient on every hero;
- glassmorphism;
- rounded floating cards;
- centered heading + three cards repeated section after section;
- huge slogan with tiny purchase action;
- all pages using copy-left/image-right hero;
- luxury signaled mainly by serif + beige/purple.

## Recognition QA

Crop out logo/header/footer and inspect Home, PLP, PDP, House, Discovery and Finder together.
PASS heuristic: at least three independent cues repeat coherently — paper/ink behavior, archive index/rules, object-study media, serif/sans hierarchy, commitment-level commerce.

## Incorrect use

- numbering every tiny component until it becomes noise;
- using violet rule as decoration with no selected/structural meaning;
- applying object-study frames to forms/checkout;
- forcing asymmetry onto utility pages where it harms completion.

## Top-of-page diversity rule
Material buyer-facing roles must not share a universal hero. Home, PLP, PDP, House/Discovery and Finder must show materially different silhouettes. Shared header/footer/tokens are allowed.

## Representative Phase 2 gate
Four roles selected because they cover the largest structural delta and critical journey:
1. Home — campaign/orientation.
2. Fragrance Library — browse/refine/merchandising.
3. PDP — evaluate/sample/full-bottle.
4. Discovery Hub — new service/journey role.

House storefront is first rollout role immediately after representative PASS and is included in Prompt 3 cross-page review.

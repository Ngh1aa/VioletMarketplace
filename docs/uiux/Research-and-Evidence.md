# Research and Evidence — Violet V4

## Evidence policy
Claims are separated as FACT, EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE, PROFESSIONAL_HYPOTHESIS or UNKNOWN. Reference behavior is not treated as proof of conversion or usability superiority.

## Project evidence
- Current source and V3 artifacts inspected from VioletMarketplace.
- OLD baseline evidence: Storefront V3 rendered artifact from Playwright at 1440 full-page plus 1280/1600 pressure captures; artifact report passed automated P0/P1 gates.
- Current source confirms static catalog, URL-persistent filters, deterministic Scent Portrait, localStorage cart and simulated checkout.

## Production reference evidence — 2026-09-09

1. Byredo — Discovery Set / La Découverte Sur-Mesure. FACT: curated 2ml sets and a voucher redeemable against later 50/100ml fragrance purchases. Transferable principle: discovery should reduce commitment risk and reconnect to full-bottle conversion.
2. Le Labo — Discovery. FACT: discovery sizes/sets are first-class navigation; some sets include value redeemable toward a full bottle. Transferable principle: sampling is a commerce format, not supporting copy.
3. Frédéric Malle — Discovery. FACT: discovery navigation includes mood, Olfactive Map, perfumers, samples and customizable discovery trio with value redemption. Transferable principle: mood and expert framing can coexist with product-first commerce.
4. Nose Paris — diagnostic FAQ. FACT: diagnostic flow recommends five samples; users can evaluate samples and later recommendations can change. Transferable principle: assisted discovery should continue after sampling.
5. Luckyscent — Fragrance Fitting / samples / discovery sets. FACT: custom fitting uses shopper input to create six handpicked samples; marketplace filtering exposes brand, main note, style, concentration, country and sample formats. Transferable principle: a multi-house marketplace can make expert curation and sampling central to trust.
6. ZGO Perfumery — Discovery Sets. FACT: multi-house niche retailer positions official-retailer authenticity, expert curation and complimentary discovery vials as service signals. Transferable principle: marketplace luxury depends on retailer authority as well as brand aesthetics.
7. Aesop — Fragrance Finder. FACT: finder sequence uses fragrance family, aroma and mood. Transferable principle: olfactory assistance can be short and concrete without personality pseudo-science.
8. Diptyque — Fragrance / Discovery. FACT: online fragrance purchases can include a trial dose so the customer can test before opening the bottle; fragrance exploration is organized by olfactory family and sensory storytelling. Transferable principle: sampling, returns and olfactory education can be integrated into premium service.

## Evidence-backed diagnosis of V3
- Strong first-screen editorial composition and coherent color/type grammar.
- Weakness is not basic polish; it is structural depth: homepage over-explains the brand, product imagery still carries placeholder/stock provenance, house identity is absent as a journey, sampling is mostly a generic Discovery SKU, and Scent Portrait ends at recommendation rather than continuing into wear/evaluation.
- PLP and product card system remain recognizably ecommerce-grid driven despite editorial treatment.

## Research limits
- No private analytics, customer interviews or conversion data are available; business-impact claims remain hypotheses.
- Remote reference sites are used as production behavior evidence and design principles only; no proprietary surface will be copied.
- Current product imagery is prototype media, not evidence of a final licensed campaign system.

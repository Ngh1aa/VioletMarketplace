## Production reference evidence

### Byredo
Source: `https://www.byredo.com/us_en/c/perfume`
Observed: product-led fragrance catalogue, `Try-It-first` attached to eligible perfumes, Fragrance Finder inserted inside commerce discovery, Discovery Sets sold beside full-size products.
Confidence: high for visible current commerce structure.

### Le Labo
Sources:
- `https://www.lelabofragrances.com/discovery.html`
- `https://www.lelabofragrances.com/fine-fragrances/discovery-set`
Observed: Discovery is a first-class navigation/format; discovery sets and sizes sit within the fragrance system rather than an isolated marketing quiz; personalization can occur at product level.
Confidence: high.

### Frédéric Malle
Source: `https://www.fredericmalle.com/discovery`
Observed: browse-by-mood taxonomy; Olfactive Map; samples/travel/discovery grouped under “Where to begin”; customizable Discovery Trio can redeem its value toward a later perfume purchase.
Confidence: high for current discovery architecture.

### Nose Paris
Sources:
- `https://noseparis.com/en/le-diagnostic-olfactif-nose`
- `https://noseparis.com/en/faq`
Observed: diagnosis → five recommendations → sample kit → evaluation → refined recommendations/favorites. This is a persistent learning loop, not a quiz dead-end.
Confidence: high for described service; Violet must not copy Nose’s AI/perfumer claims.

### Luckyscent
Sources:
- `https://www.luckyscent.com/samples`
- `https://www.luckyscent.com/brands/luckyscent-sample-packs`
Observed: sampling is marketplace infrastructure; individual fragrance samples and thematic staff/sample packs reduce commitment and catalogue overload.
Confidence: high.

### ZGO Perfumery
Sources:
- `https://zgoperfumery.com/`
- `https://zgoperfumery.com/collections/discovery-sets`
- `https://zgoperfumery.com/pages/the-zgo-discovery-vials`
Observed: multi-house perfumery foregrounds official-retailer trust, expert curation, discovery vials and discovery sets; house discovery is core merchandising.
Confidence: high.

### Diptyque
Source: `https://www.diptyqueparis.com/en_us/p/fragrances/discovery-sets/set-of-3-eaux-de-parfum-pre-composed-1.html`
Observed: curated small-set format and direct add-to-bag commerce; useful as a set-composition reference, not as multi-house IA proof.
Confidence: medium-high.

## Evidence limitations

- No analytics, customer interviews or conversion data are available. Audience/task conclusions are professional hypotheses grounded in domain patterns and current product intent, not user-research findings.
- Production references prove what those sites currently expose, not that a pattern increases conversion or satisfies accessibility.
- V4 imagery remains prototype media unless separately sourced/licensed and verified.
- Mobile/tablet evidence is intentionally outside declared scope.

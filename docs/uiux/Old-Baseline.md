## Visual findings

### Homepage
KEEP: confident fragrance-specific hero; restrained header; clear Scent Portrait entry; coherent ivory/aubergine/lilac base.
IMPROVE: too many sections explain Violet’s philosophy; `Violet method` + manifesto + family grid + three product/gallery rhythms + concierge + trust strip make the page read like a concept case study. Product grids repeat the same 3-column silhouette. Brand value is described more often than demonstrated through maisons, objects and discovery behavior.

### Fragrance Library
KEEP: persistent refinement rail, useful family/mood/presence/concentration logic, shareable URL state.
IMPROVE: large explanatory hero delays browsing; all products receive equal visual weight; card bodies are text-dense; the gallery has little merchandising rhythm or maison interruption; sample eligibility is not visible enough as a decision signal.

### PDP
KEEP: large product object; truthful price/inventory language; notes, mood and provenance; related-scent rationale.
IMPROVE: the right column behaves like a dossier with many similarly weighted sections before/around purchase; sample path is secondary and generic; commerce controls are visually small relative to the object; house authority is weak; one image carries the entire product story; related products revert to the same catalogue-card grammar.

### Scent Portrait
KEEP: strongest differentiated interaction in V3; reversible multi-step flow; transparent deterministic recommendation boundary.
IMPROVE: left story panel is oversized relative to the active decision; results terminate at product recommendations instead of a sample/evaluation loop; the experience is not yet integrated with a dedicated discovery route.

### Bag / Checkout
KEEP: quiet, low-noise commerce and explicit simulated-payment reality.
IMPROVE: only shared visual alignment is expected in V4; do not turn these utility pages into editorial showcases.

## Baseline conclusion

V3 is technically stable and visually coherent, but the redesign delta for V4 must be structural. A color/font/image-only refresh is explicitly insufficient.

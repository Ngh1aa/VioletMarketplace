## Adopted design-intelligence synthesis
1. Product/media authority over decorative surfaces.
2. Restrained functional chrome around high-impact editorial compositions.
3. Asymmetry belongs to selected page roles, not every page.
4. Serif/sans contrast is retained, but “luxury” is not outsourced to a font import.
5. Violet color identity becomes rarer and more intentional, not a page-wide wash.

No retrieved candidate is treated as proof of conversion, accessibility or production quality.

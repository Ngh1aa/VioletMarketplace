# Content Strategy — Violet V4

## Principle
Luxury should be demonstrated through product/media/service evidence, not repeated adjectives. Reduce manifesto copy and move detail to the page where it helps a decision.

## Home
First screen: one clear campaign/product world + two routes: guided discovery and fragrance browse.
Below: concise olfactive entry, one curated edit, one house story, one discovery ritual. Avoid stacking multiple philosophical explanation blocks.

## PLP
Card priority:
1. house;
2. fragrance name;
3. concentration / size;
4. 2–3 key notes or short scent phrase;
5. price;
6. sample/discovery cue only when truthful.
Remove long character paragraphs from every card. Use editorial interruptions for house/mood/discovery education.

## PDP
Above fold:
- house / fragrance;
- one-sentence scent character;
- concentration / size;
- price;
- sample/discovery versus full-bottle choice;
- primary add action.
After fold:
- scent + note architecture;
- how it wears;
- maison/provenance;
- Violet curation rationale;
- nearby directions with reasons.
Story should support choice, not delay commerce.

## Houses
Each house page needs: one recognizable house thesis, provenance/status, a distinct image/composition treatment, collection and one reason Violet carries it. Do not fabricate founder/history facts beyond prototype data; fictional house story must be clearly authored project content.

## Discovery
Explain a simple ritual:
1. choose / receive a small set;
2. wear one scent at a time on skin;
3. record reaction after drydown;
4. revisit full bottle.
If local evaluation is implemented, label it prototype/local behavior.

## Scent Portrait
Keep questions concrete: family, mood, presence, occasion. Avoid personality/identity inference. Results explain fit and lead to sample/discovery before full-bottle commitment.

## Trust language
Prefer specific: `Curated house`, `prototype sample path`, `seller / maison`, `discovery format`, `simulated checkout` where applicable. Avoid unsupported `verified`, `official`, `authenticity guaranteed` unless the project truth explicitly models that status.

## Language
Vietnamese primary with concise English brand phrases where established. Do not mix languages inside one decision label when a Vietnamese equivalent is clearer.

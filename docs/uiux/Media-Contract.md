# Media Contract — Violet V4

## Status
Applicable. Violet is media-heavy and product/house credibility depends materially on image quality, crop and provenance.

## Truth boundary
Current remote imagery is prototype/reference media. V4 may art-direct, replace and crop it, but must not claim final licensed campaign ownership. Visible unrelated real-brand logos/labels are disallowed.

## Global image grammar
- Lighting: soft side light with warm ivory highlight and violet/aubergine shadow, not neon purple.
- Materials: glass, paper, stone, silk, warm wood, quiet botanicals, skin when ethically/visually appropriate.
- Background complexity: low; product/object must read at a glance.
- Saturation: restrained; avoid applying one heavy filter that destroys image fidelity.
- Negative space: intentional and composition-owned, not empty loading/fallback blocks.
- No default flowers-around-every-bottle luxury cliché.
- Do not use the same exact image for two products visible in one shortlist/shelf unless the design explicitly represents a set/family and labels that relationship.

## Delivery
- Remote images should request bounded dimensions/quality where provider supports it.
- QA must force lazy images to load/decode before evidence capture; an image that is `complete` but `naturalWidth=0`, blank or visually unloaded is a blocker.
- New V4 local assets, if added, must remain reasonably compressed and attributable/licensed for prototype use.

## Phase 2 acceptance
No P0/P1: broken media, unrelated brand labels, accidental focal crop, stretched image, duplicate visible product imagery, blank loading areas in final screenshot evidence.

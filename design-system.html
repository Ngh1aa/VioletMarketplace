V.01 / SYSTEMViolet V4

Warm paper · archive indexing · object-first commerce

Design system for a private perfume archive.

Violet balances sensorial editorial expression with familiar commerce decisions. The system uses paper fields, aubergine rules, serif fragrance language and sparse utility controls so the bottle remains the first visual anchor.

01 / Color roles

Paper carries the experience. Violet acts as ink.

Color is assigned by role rather than decoration. Large surfaces stay warm and tactile; aubergine owns commitment, while electric violet is reserved for focus and wayfinding.

02 / Typography

Serif for scent. Sans for decisions.

Display language carries atmosphere and memory. UI copy remains familiar and precise. Micro labels are classification devices, not decoration.

03 / Rhythm & grid

A 12-column archive with deliberate pauses.

Spacing separates narrative phases rather than creating empty spectacle. Asymmetric 5/7, 4/8 and 7/5 splits keep the system editorial without sacrificing alignment.
